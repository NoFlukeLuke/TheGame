# Dice

`white1.png` to `white6.png`, `red1.png` to `red6.png` and the throw sound
(`assets/sfx/dice-throw.mp3`, from `dieThrow1.ogg`) are from
**Boardgame Pack v2 by Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), CC0 (public domain).
Downloaded from opengameart.org/content/boardgame-pack. Renamed; the sound was
re-encoded to mp3 and its level matched to the game's other sounds.

A white die is a positive face, a red die a negative one (js/dice-sim.js).
