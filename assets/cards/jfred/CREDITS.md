# Card face credits

The playing card faces (`faces.png`) are cut from **Pixel Cards** by Jfred,
https://jfredd.itch.io/pixel-cards, released under CC0 (public domain dedication):
the blank card (`stock.png`, the base of every card), the corner index and the centre
art of each of the 52 cards, and the Joker, which the game uses for its wild card. `tools/cards/build.js` makes the sheet.
