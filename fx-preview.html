1 · SCORE POPS

Numbers leaving a card or Trick and flying into PIPS / MULT. Same flight time as today by default — the presets change size and contrast, not speed.

2 · TIME CHARGE

A cost over the clock bar: swells past full size, wobbles, then shrinks away. Size is a multiple of the bar height.

3 · FOCUS

Same engine as the time charge, positive tint, no unit — just +3. Rises off the meter instead of sitting over it.

4 · CREDITS

One coin at a time, fast. Gain drops in from above with a rising blip; spend falls out of the bottom with the same blip pitched unnaturally low.
