SCORE MERGE PREVIEW

How PIPS × MULT × FOCUS become the hand's score and reach SCORE. Each idea runs the whole beat, from three chips landed on their totals to the SCORE climbing.

IDEAS

What ships: all three chips jitter together and slide into one chip reading the score; a smaller copy peels off to SCORE; the number climbs.
Owner's idea. PIPS and MULT jitter and combine into one chip (pips × mult). Then that chip and FOCUS jitter and merge into the score. Two beats, so the multiplication is read twice.
PIPS and MULT crash together with a flash and the product pops out bigger. FOCUS then drops onto it like a stamp: the chip is pressed, shakes, and the number multiplies up under it. One extra press per extra Focus application.
The three chips stay put. A line under the row writes the sum out as it happens: 40 × 6 = 240, then × 1.5 = 360, each step with its chip's jitter, and the finished line condenses into the fused chip and leaves for SCORE.

FOCUS APPLIED MORE THAN ONCE (shipped, r571)

Set Focus applies above 1. The first application is the ordinary beat; each extra one is a bigger thump on the chip and the bar with a rising sound (sfxFocusAgain(k) in the game, named here as text).
