Proposed

Where the height comes from. The column is a fixed stack of percentages of a 747×420 stage and it has to end at 97.98%, so every percent the chip row gains has to come off something else. Only five panels have slack: the SCORE/GOAL row, the knack strip, the hand preview, the credits bar and the buttons. The trick tray is not one of them — its chips are already 79px in a 77px content box (r386), so a pixel off it clips every tile. The deficit is shared across the five in proportion to what each can spare, and the table goes red the moment one would be pushed under its floor.

Two things ride these numbers and would have to move with them: the Flow reward banner (css/flow-rewards.css takes the chip row’s own box) and Poker Squares’ piece hand (css/squares.css starts where the knack band starts).
