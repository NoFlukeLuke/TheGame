HOW DO YOU WANT TO BE PAID

Fill a 5x5 board over four turns. Every row and every column is a hand.
SCORE ALL
At the end of the round all ten lines pay, worst hand first. Every card counts twice, once for its row and once for its column. Nothing to decide, everything to build.
SELECT SCORE
Each turn you cash one line and it locks for the rest of the round. On the last turn you cash 3. Fewer lines pay, so you choose when a line is finished enough to spend.
