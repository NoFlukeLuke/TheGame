THE PICK

The board comes back after the payout so one card can be picked off it. This is the un-explode: the goal finale's blast played backwards, nearest the centre landing first. Tune it here, then Dump and paste the block into js/payout-pick.js. A fresh load of this page dumps a block byte-identical to the shipped one - keep it that way.

Tap a card to see the operation bar the game shows. Explode is the real finale's geometry (200-340px out, +-160deg, scale .82, 900ms) so the two can be compared directly.
