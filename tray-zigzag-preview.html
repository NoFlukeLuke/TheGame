TRICK TRAY · ZIGZAG

Portrait tray, drawn at phone size with the game's own tiles. Each Trick sits one step right of the last and alternates up and down, so a line through their centres zigzags. The tiles in the upper row sit on top and cover the shutter (the top part) of the tiles in the lower row. No tilt. Tap a tile to hop it.
The game has this now (Settings → Display → Trick tray on a phone). The sliders here still show the layout; the game's hop is the newer one (slides out from under its neighbour, comes forward toward the middle, holds 2s). The game's numbers are in dev → HUD & Display → Zigzag tray.
