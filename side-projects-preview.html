SIDE PROJECTS · a preview

Nothing in the game changes yet. A side project is two linked rounds (an account, priority, audit or 1-on-1), the second in the next slot. About 1 quarter in 4 has one, 2 a run at most. Book the first: its cost starts. Book and clear the second: the cost ends and it pays. Walk away after the first: the cost you paid, plus a penalty. Never book the first: nothing happens.

COSTS · while the side project runs

They start when you book the first half and end when you clear the second. The short form is what the tile would print.

REWARDS · for clearing both

Paid when the second half is cleared.

PENALTIES · for walking away after the first half

On top of the cost already paid. Leaving the second half's slot without booking it walks away.
