DOORS: three looks for "the next three stops on this floor"

A look only, nothing is wired. Each screen is the board's box at desktop size. Tap a door to pick it, OPEN to walk through, VIEW SCHEDULE for the whole floor, and UP A FLOOR for the lift ride between quarters. Floors stand in for quarters here (FLOOR 2 = Q2); the game's numbers are placeholders.

Every look reads the same three stops.

Three door frames side by side, numbered like rooms. The picked door is lit; OPEN swings it. The plainest read: it is the to-do list's row of three, drawn as doors.
