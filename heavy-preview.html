HEAVY PASS

A/B for the six sounds you hear most. NOW is exactly what the game plays today; HEAVY is the retune - less high inharmonic metal, more body, sub and saturation. Both run through the real buses, ducking, saturation, limiter and room, so what you hear here is what the game would play.

Centroid is where the sound's energy actually sits in the spectrum - the number behind the word "tinkly". High is the share of the sound's energy above 1.2kHz - how much of it is sparkle rather than body. Peak and RMS are there to prove the heavy version is not just louder: RMS is held within 15% of the original, and goes red past that, because otherwise the comparison is rigged. The reference line under each table is High Roller and Lounge measured on the same sound - the target is to land near them, not at zero.
